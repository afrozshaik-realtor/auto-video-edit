Reel Studio

Privacy Policy

Effective 23 October 2026

In short:

  • Reel Studio is a private tool for a small team. It has no servers or database of its own.
  • Your reels, videos and tracking data stay in the Google Drive folder and Google Sheet the owner shares with you.
  • To edit videos automatically, clips are processed by Anthropic (Claude), and one still frame is sent to OpenAI to make a thumbnail.
  • We don’t sell data, show ads, use analytics or tracking cookies, or use your Google data to train AI models.

Who we are

Reel Studio (“the app”, “we”) is a web app at afrozshaik-realtor.github.io/auto-video-edit, run by Afroz Shaik (“the owner”). It helps a small team produce short-form videos (“reels”) for AM Builders, TCE (Real Estate) and Dr Cyber. Only people the owner invites can use it. Questions about this policy go to afroz.shaik.realtor@gmail.com.

What the app accesses

When you sign in with Google, the app asks for permission to use:

Google permission What the app does with it
Your name and email address (read from your Google Drive account details) Shows who is signed in, and records who ticked a step or changed a reel (for example “Done by …”).
Google Drive (see, edit, create and delete your Drive files) Works only inside the shared Reels folder. It creates reel folders, moves them between the Raw, Working and Completed folders, reads raw clips and B-roll, and saves finished videos, thumbnails and project files. It does not open, list or change files outside that folder, and it never deletes files.
Google Sheets (see, edit, create and delete your spreadsheets) Reads and writes the Reel Tracker sheet: reels, checklist ticks, notes, captions, businesses, tags and an activity log. It does not open any other spreadsheet.

Google only offers the Drive permission for all of your Drive, so it covers all your files. The app uses it only for the shared Reels folder: it has to move folders that teammates created and read clips uploaded through the Drive website.

Where your data is kept

  • Google Drive and Google Sheets. All reels, videos, captions, thumbnails, notes and activity are stored in the owner’s Reels folder and Reel Tracker sheet, under Google’s terms. Reel Studio has no servers or database of its own.
  • Your browser. While you’re signed in, your Google access token (it expires after about an hour) is kept in your browser’s session storage and cleared when you close the tab. Your light or dark theme choice is kept in local storage. The app sets no cookies.

The automated AI editor

When a raw clip is added to a reel, an automated job edits it. The job runs on GitHub Actions and signs in to Google as the owner’s account. It:

  • downloads that reel’s raw clip and its B-roll files, and reads the reel’s title, business, tags, style rules and any notes from the team;
  • sends them to Anthropic’s Claude, which edits the video and writes the caption and the thumbnail prompt. The video, its speech and the transcript are processed to make the edit;
  • sends one still frame of the presenter and an image prompt to OpenAI’s Image API to draw the thumbnail;
  • may send short search terms (never your video) to Pexels to find free stock footage;
  • saves the results to the reel’s folder in Drive and updates the Reel Tracker sheet.

The job’s working copies are deleted when it finishes; GitHub erases the machine after every run. Because this project’s code is public, the job’s logs are public too, so they record only reel ID numbers and progress. They never contain titles, transcripts, captions, images or video.

Google API Services: Limited Use

Reel Studio’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically:

  • Google user data is used only to provide the app’s features described above, which you can see in the app.
  • It is transferred to others only to provide those features (the AI editing and thumbnail services above), to comply with the law, or as part of a merger or sale with notice to users.
  • It is never sold, used for advertising, or used to determine creditworthiness or for lending.
  • It is never used to develop, improve or train general AI or machine-learning models. Anthropic and OpenAI receive it through their business APIs, whose terms say API data isn’t used to train their models by default.
  • No person reads it except the team members it is shared with, or the owner when you ask for help, when it’s needed for security, or when the law requires it.

How long data is kept

Reels, videos and tracking data stay in the Reels folder and Reel Tracker sheet until the owner deletes them. The app itself never deletes files. Anthropic and OpenAI may keep API inputs and outputs for a limited time under their own policies, for example to detect abuse.

Your choices

  • Sign out from the account menu in the app. This removes your access token from the browser.
  • Revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions by removing Reel Studio.
  • Ask for a copy, a correction or deletion of data about you (for example your name in the activity log) by emailing the owner. Leaving the team ends your access when the owner stops sharing the folder with you.

Security

All connections use HTTPS. The app’s credentials for the AI editor are kept encrypted in GitHub, never in the code. Only the owner can change them. Access to reels is controlled by Google Drive sharing.

Children

Reel Studio is a work tool for adults and isn’t directed at children under 13.
